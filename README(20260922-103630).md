# AI Wealth Navigator

> **Before You Spend, Save, Invest, or Build — See the Impact.**

AI Wealth Navigator is an AI-powered financial wellness and decision-support platform designed to help users understand their financial position, explore "what-if" scenarios, understand the potential impact of financial and lifestyle decisions, and review explainable recommendations.

## 1. Problem Statement

Traditional financial tools mainly display balances, expenses, charts and investment information. Users can see their financial data, but often struggle to understand the potential consequences of a decision.

Examples include:

- Can I afford a ₹10,000 vacation?
- What happens if I save ₹5,000 more every month?
- How can a purchase affect my financial goal?
- What changes when I choose a different risk scenario?
- Should I explore a growth opportunity or a startup idea?

AI Wealth Navigator addresses this gap by combining financial understanding, scenario simulation, AI-assisted explanations and human-controlled recommendations in one platform.

## 2. Solution

The platform follows a simple decision-support flow:

**Understand → Explore → Simulate → Ask AI → Understand Impact → Take Action**

Users can:

- View their financial position.
- Create and track financial goals.
- Simulate savings and spending changes.
- Understand lifestyle spending impact.
- Explore illustrative risk and investment scenarios.
- Explore synthetic startup concepts through Invest vs Build.
- Ask an AI Wealth Assistant "what-if" questions.
- Review AI-generated recommendations.
- Approve, modify or reject suggested actions.

## 3. Key Features

### Financial Dashboard
Provides an overview of income, essential expenses, savings, emergency fund, available capacity and goals.

### Goal Planner
Allows users to define financial goals, track progress and understand projected timelines.

### What-If Simulator
Lets users change savings, spending or other scenario variables and observe the potential impact.

### Lifestyle Impact
Connects everyday decisions such as vacations, dining, shopping and entertainment with financial capacity and goals.

### Risk & Investment Explorer
Provides illustrative scenarios across conservative, moderate and higher-risk profiles with assumptions and uncertainty.

### Startup Lab
Allows users to explore synthetic startup concepts and compare an illustrative **Invest vs Build** path.

### AI Wealth Assistant
Provides simulated conversational guidance for financial "what-if" questions and explains potential impact, assumptions and trade-offs.

### Action Plan
Provides human-controlled recommendation handling through:

**Approve | Modify | Reject**

## 5. AI / Agentic AI Approach

The target AI architecture uses a multi-step workflow:

```text
User Question
      ↓
AI Orchestrator
      ↓
Financial Analysis Agent
      ↓
Goal Analysis Agent
      ↓
Scenario Agent
      ↓
Recommendation Agent
      ↓
Explanation Agent
      ↓
Human Review
      ↓
Approve / Modify / Reject
```

The agents have focused responsibilities:

- **Financial Analysis Agent** — understands income, expenses, savings and financial capacity.
- **Goal Analysis Agent** — evaluates goals, progress and timelines.
- **Scenario Agent** — evaluates "what-if" changes.
- **Recommendation Agent** — combines outputs into decision-support options.
- **Explanation Agent** — communicates assumptions, uncertainty and trade-offs.

**Core principle: AI recommends. Human decides.**

## 11. Responsible AI

AI Wealth Navigator is designed as a decision-support platform.

Important principles:

- The hackathon uses synthetic/demo financial data.
- Scenario results are illustrative.
- Assumptions should be visible to the user.
- AI recommendations should be explainable.
- Uncertainty should be communicated.
- Users can approve, modify or reject recommendations.
- The system does not automatically execute financial transactions.
- Startup Lab concepts are synthetic hackathon concepts and are not real investment offers.
- The platform does not guarantee financial outcomes.

## 12. Current MVP and Future Architecture

The current hackathon MVP focuses on the complete frontend experience using synthetic data and simulated AI behavior.

The architecture is designed to evolve toward:

**React → API Gateway → Lambda → DynamoDB/S3 → Amazon Bedrock**

This separation allows the user experience to remain stable while backend services and managed GenAI capabilities are added.
