PROJECT

AI Wealth Navigator is an AI-powered financial wellness platform designed to help users understand their financial position, explore what-if scenarios, evaluate the impact of financial decisions, and receive explainable next-best-action recommendations.

NOTE

The current hackathon MVP uses synthetic/sample financial data and simulated AI behavior. The AWS and Amazon Bedrock components shown in the architecture represent the target production-ready architecture.

RESPONSIBLE AI

The platform is designed to keep the user in control. AI recommendations are explainable, assumptions are visible, and users can approve, modify, or reject suggested actions. The platform does not automatically execute financial transactions.
